# Museum, Editorial & Institutional Design Reference

This guide covers design aesthetics, typographic systems, catalog layouts, and reading experiences for museum archives, contemporary art galleries, architectural monographs, and editorial publications.

## 1. Curatorial Aesthetic DNA
- Archival Warm Paper: Soft alabaster (#FBF9F5), warm limestone, antique charcoal text.
- Generous negative space with delicate 1px hairline borders.
- Curatorial accents: Terracotta, Deep Lapis, Bronze, Forest Ink.

## 2. Typographic Architecture & Reading Measure
- Headings: High-contrast editorial serif (Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, Instrument Serif).
- Body Measure: Strictly constrained to 65–75 characters per line (max-w-prose, approx. 640px–720px) with relaxed leading (1.75–1.85).
- Elegant initial drop caps for chapter openings.

## 3. Catalog Layout Patterns
- Accession Detail View: Museum accession numbering, medium, dimensions, and provenance list.
- Editorial 3-Tier Salience: Lead story, secondary features, department archive rows.
- Two-Column Asymmetric Reading Grid: 70% main essay body with 30% floating margin notes.
