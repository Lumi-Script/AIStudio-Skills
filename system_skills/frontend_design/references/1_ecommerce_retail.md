# E-Commerce & Retail Design Guidelines

This reference covers digital storefronts, product catalogs, marketplaces, and
luxury direct-to-consumer experiences.

## 1. Scope & Taxonomy Coverage
- Furniture, Lighting & Interiors
- Food & Drink Producers
- Audio, Instruments & Hardware
- Beauty & Fragrance
- Watches & Jewellery
- Publishing & Books
- Sport, Clubs & Athletes
- Automotive & Mobility Retail

## 2. Architectural & Visual Rules
### A. Layout Structure & Section Discipline
- **Storefront Hero**: 1 bold campaign focal point with a direct route into the featured collection.
- **Featured Collection Grid**: 3-column (desktop) product grid with generous whitespace.
- **Contiguous Purchase Module (PDP)**: On product detail views, keep product title, price, selected variant, availability, and primary buy CTA in one stable contiguous purchase module.
- **Section Ceiling**: Max 3–4 sections on the homepage.

### B. Product Cards & Visual Hierarchy
- Lead with high-impact product photography on clean neutral backdrops.
- Crisp metadata, tabular pricing, subtle hover micro-interactions.
- No badge spam: maximum 1 subtle text tag.

### C. Promotional Restraint & Checkout Trust
- At most one slim, dismissible top banner (≤ 40px). Never fire discount popups on entry.
- Clear payment terms, delivery thresholds, and order confirmation states.

## 3. Typography & Color Strategies
- Luxury: High-contrast serif display paired with clean refined body text.
- Streetwear: Bold geometric sans display paired with clean body text.
- Industrial: Technical mono numbers and structured sans.

## 4. Anti-Patterns to Avoid
- No Cluttered Flash Sale Clones
- No Missing Pricing or Cart Feedback
- No Unresponsive Grid Wraps
