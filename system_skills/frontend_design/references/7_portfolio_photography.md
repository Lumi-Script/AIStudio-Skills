# Portfolio & Photography Design Guidelines

This reference covers creative portfolios, video editing showreels, photography galleries, writer brainstorm tools, and student achievement showcases.

## 1. Media-First Masonry & Bento Grid
- Dynamic Bento Box: Wide showcase cards (col-span-2) paired with compact thumbnails.
- Minimalist Framing: Monochromatic off-white or stark black canvas letting visual work shine.
- Fullscreen Lightbox Modal: High-resolution media viewer with metadata and ESC key closure.

## 2. Editorial Typographic Impact
- Oversized extra-bold headings paired with refined serif body text.
- 3-in-a-row student profile cards with clean unboxed achievement metrics.
- No ghost watermark words behind content.
