# Landing Pages & Marketing Design Guidelines

This reference covers corporate landing pages, creative agency showcases, lead
generation funnels, product drops, and B2B service websites.

## 1. Scope & Taxonomy Coverage
- Creative & Digital Marketing Agencies
- Automotive & Mobility Marketing
- Hardware & Manufacturing OEM/ODM
- Product Drops & SaaS Pre-launches
- Lead Generation & Service Portfolios

## 2. Architectural & Visual Rules
### A. Section Flow & Visual Storytelling
Proposition → Mechanism / Capabilities → Proof / Evidence → Conversion / Action

1. **Hero Section**: Single dominant focal carrier with an unmistakable headline and single primary CTA.
2. **Core Capabilities**: Asymmetric bento-grid or numbered service list detailing real business outcomes.
3. **Proof of Impact**: 2–3 project cards with concrete, quantified metrics (+140% Qualified Inbound Leads).
4. **Interactive Lead Capture**: Minimalist form with clean input borders and immediate feedback.
5. **Quiet Footer**: Wordmark, navigation mirror, legal notices, contact info.

### B. Copywriting & CTA System
- Ban generic SaaS buzzwords ("supercharge", "empower", "unlock").
- Single-line action labels: "Schedule Discovery Call", "Request Manufacturing Quote".
- At most one primary CTA per decision block.

### C. Bento-Grid & Asymmetric Hierarchy
- Use dynamic column spans (col-span-2 with col-span-1) to emphasize marquee capabilities.
- Inner surfaces use subtle hairline borders rather than heavy drop shadows.
